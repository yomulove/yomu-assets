03 · Where change begins

With the adult.

A nervous system on alert has a harder time learning. And an overwhelmed adult has fewer resources to help a group regulate.

That's why Mindfulness YOMU starts with the adult. Because regulation is also taught through how we breathe, how we respond and how we show up in the classroom.

04 · What is YOMU

Mindfulness that is simple, practical and within every teacher's reach.

Mindfulness YOMU

With more than 10 years of experience, a mindfulness system built on music and on simple, powerful tools that adults bring to children and teens. No prior experience needed, and from the very first week there are tools teachers can use in the classroom.

YOMU Institute

A center for research, knowledge and education that brings together experts in the five pillars of the YOMU System: neuroscientists, psychologists, educators, and breathwork and music specialists. It researches, trains and certifies the adults who support children and teens.

Our mission

To give children, preteens and teens, and the adults who support them, practical, simple and powerful tools to cultivate awareness, emotional regulation and well-being. We believe that growing healthy, resilient and happy minds and hearts is everyone's right. It all begins with ourselves.

Our vision
